# Attribution And License Notes

`manuscript-workflow` is an original local skill created for evidence-first scientific manuscript construction, audit, rewriting, and final packaging.

Its workflow design was informed by high-level patterns observed in these public repositories:

- PaperSpine by WUBING2023: https://github.com/WUBING2023/PaperSpine
- Academic Research Skills by Imbad0202 and contributors: https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills
- Academic Research Skills Codex adapter: https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills-codex

No upstream source files, prompts, examples, or templates are vendored in this repository. The local skill re-expresses general workflow ideas such as motivation-first framing, material grounding, claim verification, reviewer-style audit, and manuscript packaging in its own structure and wording.
